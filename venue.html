The space
A dairy barn built in 1939, restored as an event space on five acres, with a garden for outdoor ceremonies and an upper-level deck. Climate controlled, so April weather is not a problem.
Parking
Free on-site parking. Rideshare drops at the main entrance.
Accessibility
The venue is accessible, and the ceremony garden is reached over level ground. Tell us on your RSVP if there is anything we can arrange to make the evening easier for you.
Dress code
Cocktail attire. Part of the evening is on grass and gravel — block heels or flats will serve you better than stilettos, and April evenings in Virginia get cool once the sun drops, so bring a layer.
