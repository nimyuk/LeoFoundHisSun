The Day

One venue, one evening — no shuttling between locations. Everything below happens at Sweeney Barn.

    Ceremony

    In the garden if the weather cooperates, inside the barn if it does not. Short and sincere.

  3. 5:00 pm

  4. 6:15 pm

    Dinner & toasts

    Dancing

    Cake, first dances, and then the floor is yours.

    Last song & send-off

    We will say goodbye properly, we promise.
