How we got here

Our Story

We met the way most good things start: unplanned, and slightly late. What was supposed to be one coffee turned into a walk that lasted the rest of the afternoon, and then into the sort of ordinary, easy days that quietly add up to a life.

Since then we have shared apartments and road trips, arguments about how to load a dishwasher, a great many bad movies, and a few genuinely hard years that we came out of holding on tighter than before. Somewhere in all of it, the question stopped being if and became when.

Now it is April 2027, at a 1939 dairy barn in Virginia, with the small group of people who made us who we are. Thank you for being one of them.

Dress code
Cocktail attire. Part of the evening is on grass and gravel — block heels or flats will serve you better than stilettos, and April evenings in Virginia get cool once the sun drops, so bring a layer.

Good questions

FAQ

What time should I arrive?

Doors open at 4:00 pm and the ceremony begins at 4:30 pm. Arriving by 4:15 gives you time to park and settle without rushing.

What is the dress code?

Cocktail attire. Suits or a jacket, dresses or separates — dressy but not black tie. Some of the evening is outdoors on grass, so heels that sink are worth avoiding, and April evenings here cool off quickly.

Is the ceremony indoors or outdoors?

We are planning an outdoor ceremony in the garden. If April decides otherwise, everything moves inside the barn, which is climate controlled. Either way you will be comfortable.

Can I bring a plus one?

Our guest list is around 60 people, so we can only accommodate the names listed on your invitation. If your invitation includes a guest, the RSVP form will let you add them.

Are children invited?

No.

Is there parking?

Yes — free parking on site, right at the venue. Rideshare works well in Manassas if you would rather not drive home.

I have a dietary restriction or allergy.

There is a field for it on the RSVP form. Tell us anything at all and we will pass it to the caterer.

I am coming from out of town — where should I stay?

Manassas is about 35 miles southwest of Washington, D.C. Dulles (IAD) is the closest airport at roughly 25 minutes; Reagan National (DCA) is about 40. There are several hotels within ten minutes of the barn — the Residence Inn and Courtyard at Manassas Battlefield Park, and the Hampton Inn. We have not set up a room block, so book whatever suits you, and tell us if you would like a hand.

It is a Thursday — is anything happening the rest of the week?

Nothing formal yet. If enough of you are in town we will organise something casual the night before, and details will show up on this page.

Can I take photos during the ceremony?

We would love for you to be present with us instead — please keep phones away during the ceremony itself. After that, take everything you like, and we will share our photographer's gallery here afterwards.

Who do I contact with anything else?

Email us at kyuminleolee@gmail.com. For venue-specific questions, Sweeney Barn is at (703) 282-3033.
